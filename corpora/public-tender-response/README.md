# Public Tender Response

Synthetic knowledge base for evaluating a complete public-tender response workflow: requirement
extraction, cross-document reconciliation, capability-gap analysis, recommendation and production
of durable deliverables.

**Source**: entirely synthetic. All organisations, people, projects and identifiers are fictional.
**Sensitive data**: no.
**Validation dataset**: `validation/dataset-public-tender-response.json`.
**End-to-end scenario**: `../../use-cases/reliable-tender-work-completion/`.

## Source files

- `01-consultation-overview.md` — buyer, dates, scope and submission rules.
- `02-technical-requirements.md` — numbered functional and non-functional requirements.
- `03-evaluation-and-elimination.md` — scoring and elimination rules.
- `04-buyer-clarifications.md` — later answers that supersede parts of the initial documents.
- `05-candidate-capabilities.md` — fictional candidate's current product and delivery capabilities.
- `06-references-and-evidence.md` — references, certifications and evidence dates.
- `07-staffing-plan.csv` — proposed people, experience, availability and responsibilities.
- `08-commercial-estimate.md` — cost estimate, assumptions and exclusions.

The planted difficulty is not hidden trivia. It is the need to reconcile dated sources, apply
elimination rules, distinguish evidence from promises, and complete all requested outputs without
inventing files or links.
