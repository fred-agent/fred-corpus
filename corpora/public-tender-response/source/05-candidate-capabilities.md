# Asteria Digital — candidate capabilities

Asteria Digital is a fictional software and integration provider. Its AtlasDocs platform currently
runs in dedicated private-cloud tenants in Frankfurt and Dublin. Production data, backups and
operational logs can be pinned to Frankfurt; support personnel outside the EU can be denied
production access.

AtlasDocs supports REST APIs, SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Its standard service level is 99.95%
monthly availability, with a maximum of two hours announced maintenance. Priority-1 incidents are
acknowledged within 15 minutes and the current restoration target is three hours, 24/7.

The migration factory has demonstrated throughput of 900,000 documents per month after discovery
and mapping. It preserves folder paths, timestamps, author and configurable metadata. A 100,000
document pilot normally takes eight weeks after access to representative source data.

The current user interface was audited against WCAG 2.1 AA in November 2025. The audit reported
three remaining AA failures in keyboard focus and error identification. Product planning currently
targets their closure in August 2027, but this work has not been contractually prioritised.

Asteria operates security monitoring and Priority-1 coordination with employees of its EU entity.
It proposes to subcontract only non-production training material translation.

This capability statement describes the current position. It is not evidence that future roadmap
items, certificate renewals or staffing changes have been completed.
