# NOVA-DOC 2027 — consultation overview

**Buyer:** Novapolis Metropolitan Agency (NMA), a fictional public body  
**Reference:** NMA-DIG-2027-04  
**Publication date:** 5 January 2027  
**Submission deadline:** 12 February 2027 at 12:00 UTC  
**Contract duration:** 36 months, followed by one optional 12-month extension  
**Maximum evaluated price:** EUR 1,800,000 excluding tax for the firm 36-month scope

NMA seeks a document collaboration and search service hosted in a private European cloud. The
service will replace three legacy repositories and must migrate their documents and metadata.

The response must contain:

1. a completed compliance matrix covering every numbered requirement;
2. a technical and security response with supporting evidence;
3. a delivery plan naming responsible people and milestones;
4. a firm price for the 36-month scope;
5. CVs and references required by the elimination rules.

The planned start date is 1 April 2027. The initial document states that production deployment
must occur by 31 October 2027. Answers formally published in the buyer clarification log supersede
the initial consultation documents wherever they conflict.

Responses may identify assumptions, but an assumption cannot replace evidence explicitly required
at submission time. NMA may reject a response that is incomplete or that treats a future action as
already-completed evidence.
