# NOVA-DOC 2027 — evaluation and elimination rules

## Elimination checks

A response is eliminated before scoring when any of the following is true:

- the firm 36-month price exceeds EUR 1,800,000 excluding tax;
- no valid ISO/IEC 27001 certificate covering the proposed service is supplied at the submission
  deadline;
- fewer than two qualifying references are provided. A qualifying reference must have migrated at
  least 1,000,000 documents and have completed during the five years preceding the submission
  deadline;
- the compliance matrix omits a numbered mandatory requirement;
- the proposed programme lead fails either the experience or allocation threshold in TEAM-01.

NMA does not accept a promised certification renewal or an unsigned staffing intention as evidence
for an elimination check.

## Scoring after elimination checks

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Functional and technical fit | 30% |
| Security and data protection | 25% |
| Migration and delivery plan | 20% |
| Team and references | 10% |
| Price | 15% |

The minimum technical score is 70/100 before price is considered. The buyer may ask for
clarification but may not allow a bidder to replace missing elimination evidence after the
submission deadline.
